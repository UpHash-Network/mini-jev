# October 3 Japanese/English scope extension

The revised manuscript adds 4,800 measured calls on 240 further Japanese and 240 English five-choice questions, shared by Qwen2.5-1.5B and Phi-4-mini. The primary error-detection and 720-call allocation comparisons do not establish additional value from order-TV. All secondary scores and budgets are retained in the [separate public study bundle](../generality_20261003/publication/README.md).

The rejected Phi optimized-head gate, later synthetic diagnosis and pre-benchmark v2 amendment remain available. Both models use the stock full-position head in the measured experiment. The new gate checks repeatability, not optimized-head equivalence.

[Checks](CHECKS.json), [numerical review](../generality_20261003/publication/INDEPENDENT_NUMERICAL_REVIEW.md) and [PDF review](PDF_REVIEW.json) describe the scope. The 10-page manuscript has main text, limitations and acknowledgements through page 6, ethics/references on 7–8, and a two-page appendix on 9–10. Original experiment archives and the separately submitted arXiv files are unchanged.

AI calculation and rendering checks are not human validation. The locally prepared formative pilot has no participants or observations, and independent-machine inference remains pending.
